1. Introduction


Data-driven decision making is becoming a pivotal factor in business to grasp the performance of sales, profitability and behaviour of the market, especially with the development of ecommerce. For organisations, transactional data can be analysed to see trends from the past and evidence can guide business decisions. However if this is used as the only measure, business performance cannot be assessed as the amount of sales can be high while profitability can be relatively low which means that these cannot be used as the only financial measure. Multiple indicators of business performance involving sales, profit, products, geographical areas and performance, compared to business plans, are therefore required for effective business analysis (Enache, 2023). Explanatory data analysis also comes into very handy since it enables an understanding of the relationships and patterns in the current set of data, before turning to more sophisticated predictive techniques. The implementation of analytical methods can assist in understanding the sales behaviour in e-commerce and guide and inform decision-making regarding pricing, marketing, and operations (Islam et al., 2025). In the present project Python and Pandas will be used on a dataset from e-commerce representing a fictitious Indian e-commerce retailer. This bundles will be three related files and are List of Orders, Order details and Sales Target. The data for the order entities contain details of customer, date and geographic information, and the data in the order detail entities contain the data for sales amount, profit, quantity, category and sub-category. The provided dataset containing the data to be aimed is the monthly-forward planned sales by Category available on the target. This analysis is segmented into the following 5 business questions: Monthly revenue & profit performance, Product-category performance, sub-category profitability, geographic concentration of revenues and actual vs planned sales. These dimensions give a more comprehensive insight of the commercial realisation than revenue analysis alone.
 

2. Business Context and Analytical Objectives


The analysis is performed on a hypothetical Indian e-commerce retailer who would like to find out how their commercial performance has been in the past. The reason is to give an evidence-based description (not an estimate) of turnover, market share, operating cost or customer demographics across the company because the data was not available in the dataset provided. Business intelligence methods can help convert the transactional information into useful data that helps in making business decisions (Weber, 2023).

The analysis is based on five questions:
Q1. In what way are revenues and profits effected with time?
Q2. What is the best overall performer?
Q3. What portion of the product categories makes or breaks the profits?
Q4. Of all the states, which are the best at generating revenue?
Q5. Are actualised selling figures at a level and pace to satisfy the projected selling level?

Response to these questions intentionally investigates various facets of competition. The temporal analysis indicates whether there are fluctuations year-round, the category analysis is the comparison between the overall product categories, the sub category analysis is the comparison between the various sub categories based on their level of profitability, the geographic analysis checks the concentration of revenue by geographical region and the target analysis is analysing current results against a management goal. The presence of this combination allows a more comprehensive evaluation of the performance of the commercial products, and captures the benefits of interwoven business indicators over relying on any one indicator (Gino, 2025).
 

3. Data Exploration and Quality Assessment


Its uses has been three CSV files namely: List of Orders, Order Details and Sales Target. The initial number of rows and columns for the List of Orders was 560, the number of rows and 6 columns in the Order Details table was 1,500, and the Sales Target table had 36 rows in it and 3 columns. Order Data includes Order ID, Order Date, CustomerName and State and City. Order Details includes Order ID, Amount, Profit, Quantity, Category / Sub-Category and Sales Target includes Month of Order Date, Category / Target. It can be exploited to see relation between Order Level and Geographic/Temporal information with relation to financial and product information at Order Level. Orders has been detected to have some important quality issue. It had 560 rows but 500 rows actually had data in them – leaving 60 rows completely blank. In the populated records, there were no missing data for the five fields. There were 1,500 values not equal to null (1,500 cases) when considering details in the Order and 36 values not equal to null (cases) when considering details in the Sales Target. It was therefore decided to eliminate the 60 blank rows, rather than consider them Orders. This distinction becomes meaningful since the amount of the original data is called 560 orders, but this is too big an actual file size. It didn't auto capture repeated Order IDs in Order Details as a duplicate. This is appropriate because a single order can have multiple line items, so it can be (and ought to be) represented on multiple lines in the detail table. The notebook may even have multiple products and sub-categories for a particular Order ID, such as B-25601. Finally, before merging the datasets, the Order ID relationship was analyzed. This generated the structure of actual data which included 1,500 transaction records on a “detail” level. With this method, valid product level observations are kept and linked with the order level information. This attention to structure of data is crucial as it can affect further business analysis if the data is incorrectly duplicated or merged (Lin, 2025).

4. Data Preprocessing and Preparation


Data preparation was done in a systematic manner. The first was that copies of the raw data were kept so that they would preserve the original information prior to its modification. The entire blank order rows were then thrown away as they would have no analytical data relevant to them. Order Date (ntime): Text fields were changed to datetime format. Dayfirst=True was used due to the dates supplied are in a day-month-year format. Invalid dates checked - converted. Some other temporal variables were added, such as Year, Month, Month Name, Quarter and Day Name. These changes allow for the data to be combined at various time periods and for a common time frame when comparing them temporally. Orders data was sorted in chronological order after being cleaned. Order Details were then compared with Orders prior to merging on Order IDs. This meant that no assumptions needed to be made about row position in order to be able to trace the transaction record to the required information about the customer, location and date. These statistics were then combined to arrive at a monthly revenue, profit, and category performance and sub-category profitability and state-level revenue. A monthly time variable was also calculated to get the actual sales data to correspond with the data set being measured. This is required because this is done to record the monthly and category level sales planned for the target file. On the other hand, the target analysis demanded that actual sales data be counted back at the same time dimension.Prior, the actual sales data had to be aggregated at the same time dimension for comparison purposes. These pre processing decisions were not just mere and technical tasks. These are all transformations that make it easier to perform a specific analysis: Converting the date keys, removing blank observations, checking the relationships, preserving multiple detail rows are all such transformations. To make a reliable analytical result, the meaning and structure of the underlying data need to be preserved during the processes of preprocessing (Weber 2023).

5. Business Question 1 – Monthly Revenue and Profit Performance


How does revenue and profit change over time?


By analysing timeseries data, management can gain insights of relatively successful and unsuccessful time periods and see if there is a correlation between sales and profit. It is important to look at revenue separately from the indicators as revenue indicates generation of sales while the rest shows the money earned in the dataset (Islam et al., 2025).

10. Overall Discussion and Business Implications


The 5 analyses give the retailer a holistic perspective on his performance in the past. First up, it's notable that revenue and profitability are not synonymous. The analysis of the numbers per month reveals that there is a lot of variation in both of these figures and category analysis reveals that Electronics achieved the highest revenue figure but Clothing achieved the highest profit figure. The sub category analysis further supports this that profitability is quite different throughout the same product grouping. This substantiates the concept for using more than one performance measure in making business decisions over just sales volume (Enache, 2023).

Second, there is greater managerial detail in sub-category findings as compared to only category level findings. The overall profits are positive for Printers and Bookcases and negative for Tables and Electronic Games. This indicates that individual products need to be investigated in detail and that management should concentrate on these products, especially if they have suffered large losses. But more granular data on transactions would be needed to determine the need for repricing, restructuring or discontinuations of products/ services (Imran et al., 2025).

Thirdly, there is great concentration of revenue in Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra as was identified by the geographic analysis. A significant part of the revenue markets are these states, but little evidence is provided of the performance of these markets and why they perform that way. This means that these results should not be considered as the cause of the results but as an indicator for future research on this issue in the region (Raktate and Das, 2026).

There are also a variety of limitations to the analysis. This data is only from a single year, and it is not possible to determine trends over a longer timeframe. It does not include detailed customer details, marketing spending, discount and specific cost details. No causal modelling, prediction or machine-learning prediction was done. Thus, the results may only be read as 'explanatory descriptive evidence' and are not predictions nor explanations of causality. Ideally, additional future extensions can be made by including customer segregation, customer lifetime value, forecasting and predictive analytics of customers (Norouzi, 2024).
 

11. Conclusion


The analysis is able to address all five business questions. There were significantly more differences among monthly performances, with the highest monthly revenue being in January 2019 and only beginning to earn a profit toward the end of the observation period. Clothing had the highest Aggregate profit, Quantity generated and Electronics had the highest revenue. In sub categories, Printers contributed positively while the Tables contributed negatively, the strongest positive is Printers and the largest negative is Tables. While looking through the geographic analysis, the strength of revenue markets was found to be Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra. In the end, the actual sales in an annual period exceeded the aggregate target by ~99% although there were differences in the monthly sales activity.
