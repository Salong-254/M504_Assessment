EXPLANATORY DATA ANALYSIS OF E-COMMERCE SALES PERFORMANCE: REVENUE, PROFITABILITY, PRODUCT CATEGORIES, GEOGRAPHIC TRENDS AND SALES TARGET ACHIEVEMENT USING PYTHON



1. Introduction


Multiple indicators of business performance involving sales, profit, products, geographical areas and performance, compared to business plans, are therefore required for effective business analysis (Enache, 2023). In the present project Python and Pandas will be used on a dataset from e-commerce representing a fictitious Indian e-commerce retailer. This bundles will be three related files and are List of Orders, Order details and Sales Target. This analysis is segmented into the following 5 business questions: Monthly revenue & profit performance, Product-category performance, sub-category profitability, geographic concentration of revenues and actual vs planned sales.

2. Business Context and Analytical Objectives


The analysis is based on five questions:
Q1. In what way are revenues and profits effected with time?
Q2. What is the best overall performer?
Q3. What portion of the product categories makes or breaks the profits?
Q4. Of all the states, which are the best at generating revenue?
Q5. Are actualised selling figures at a level and pace to satisfy the projected selling level?

3. Data Exploration and Quality Assessment


Three CSV files were used: List of Orders, Order Details and Sales Target. The List of Orders had 560 rows and 500 rows actually had data in them – leaving 60 rows completely blank, which were eliminated. Order Details had 1,500 rows and 6 columns, and Sales Target had 36 rows and 3 columns. This attention to structure of data is crucial as it can affect further business analysis if the data is incorrectly duplicated or merged (Lin, 2025).

4. Data Preprocessing and Preparation

Blank order rows were removed. Order Date was converted to datetime format using dayfirst=True since dates were supplied in day-month-year format. Temporal variables (Year, Month, Month Name, Quarter, Day Name) were added and Orders data was sorted in chronological order (Weber 2023).

10. Overall Discussion and Business Implications

Revenue and profitability are not synonymous: Electronics achieved the highest revenue figure but Clothing achieved the highest profit figure (Enache, 2023). Profits are positive for Printers and Bookcases and negative for Tables and Electronic Games (Imran et al., 2025). There is great concentration of revenue in Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra (Raktate and Das, 2026). This data is only from a single year and no causal modelling or machine-learning prediction was done (Norouzi, 2024).























11. Conclusion

The analysis addresses all five business questions. Monthly revenue was highest in January 2019, with profit turning positive toward the end of the observation period. Clothing had the highest Aggregate profit and Electronics had the highest revenue. Printers was the strongest positive sub-category and Tables the largest negative. Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra were the strongest revenue markets. Actual sales exceeded the aggregate target by ~99%.
